# OpenRamble 0.27.1

A meeting recording that captured the other people and missed you now says so, and it tries the microphone the way a second app on this Mac is supposed to share it.

# What changed

- Recording a meeting while Zoom, Meet, Teams or FaceTime already has the microphone now reads the input the same way the rest of the Mac does, instead of starting a second engine that could succeed and write silence.
- If your voice still does not arrive, the orange strip, the You meter, the menu bar, VoiceOver, and the finished recording all say your microphone was not captured. Previously only a missing other side was treated that way.
- After a few seconds of silence the recorder drops a pinned input and starts again on the default microphone, once.
- A meeting whose microphone will not start still records the other side. A voice note without a microphone still refuses to start.

Recognition and transcription remain entirely local. Nothing is uploaded, and no other app is told you are recording.
