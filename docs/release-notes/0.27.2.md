# A clearer recordings window

- Copy the entire transcript while a recording is still running, including the latest transcribed words.
- Find recordings in a compact list grouped by day, without repeated Meeting labels. Recordings started within the same minute show seconds to tell them apart.
- Control a recording from one Liquid Glass bar with its time, audio levels, pause, and a clearly labelled Stop button.
- Read a quieter transcript with grouped source headings. Scroll back without losing your place as new words arrive, then use Jump to Latest to follow the conversation again.
- Use Copy Transcript and More instead of a row of unexplained icons. Save, rename, recording details, Finder, and Trash are under More.
- Play from a timestamp while keeping transcript text available for selection. Playback controls take up one compact row.
